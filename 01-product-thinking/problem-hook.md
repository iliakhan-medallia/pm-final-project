## 1. Chosen scenario

**Path:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

## 2. The strategic crisis

_The big-picture business risk. If the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?_

> If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will slow down its growth and lose customers to competitors.

## 3. The moment of misery

_The specific point where the product fails the user, forcing a manual workaround (Google, spreadsheets, group texts, a competitor)._

> The user is currently forced to ignore most of the features being built because they are distractions, and friction pushes them back to their old paths (like spreadsheets) or toward competitors who are leaner and more specialized for their use cases.

## 4. Problem hook

_One urgent sentence fusing the business risk and the user pain, your pitch for why this is the most important thing to work on right now._

> We must solve the churn and slowing adoption by addressing the high number of features that frontline coordinators don't currently use or need, and focusing on optimizing the ones they do require for their day-to-day work.

## 5. Value proposition

_Who it's for, the new value, and why it's urgent to launch now._

> For **frontline coordinators**, we will **optimize and simplify the platform** because **they are leaving us for competitors who are less complex**.
