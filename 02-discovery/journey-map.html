Future-state journey: Fleet dispatcher (night shift)

Persona 1 of Module 2. Goal: route changes and stop statuses match what happens in the field. Strategy: reclaim operational simplicity by making the platform the fastest and most reliable tool for everyday dispatch work.

1

Reassign with confirmation

User action
Reassigns the route from the dispatch board in one step.
Internal state
Change is pushed to the driver's device immediately.
Pain point addressed
10–15 minute propagation delay (BUG-2044, Critical).
  • Dispatcher reassigns the route in one step → driver is notified instantly.
  • Board shows acknowledgment status → no WhatsApp check-in needed.
2

Driver acknowledges

User action
Driver accepts the change and re-sequences stops.
Internal state
One shared route, with no WhatsApp relay.
Pain point addressed
Drivers act on stale routes and drive the wrong way (UXR-02).
  • Driver sees an alert on the device → no wrong-way driving.
  • Driver accepts re-sequenced stops → route matches the plan.
3

Monitor the live board

User action
Scans the exceptions view during the night shift.
Internal state
Stop status reflects the field in near real time.
Pain point addressed
20–60 minute status lag and false "in progress" stops (BUG-2072, Medium).
  • Dispatcher scans exceptions, not every stop → attention goes to problems.
  • Status arrives within seconds → the board can be trusted without calls.
4

Exceptions and close-out

User action
Reviews failed or pending items, then closes the shift.
Internal state
The platform is the single system of record.
Pain point addressed
Lost routes and failed uploads (BUG-2031, BUG-2050, BUG-2061).
  • Driver loses signal → route stays on the device and stops keep moving.
  • Photo uploads retry automatically → delivery records complete without follow-up.
  • End-of-shift data flows to admin reports → reporting reflects what happened.
Today: WhatsApp is the system of record Future: the platform is the system of record

Competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. One system of record. The dispatcher enters each change once, removing the WhatsApp and re-entry double work.
  2. Changes land in seconds. Reassignments reach drivers in seconds rather than 10–15 minutes, reducing wrong-route miles and recovery calls.
  3. Auditable, reportable data. Shift data reflects what actually happened, giving the admin reporting the buyer values a reliable basis.

Evidence base: UXR-01, UXR-02, UXR-03, UXR-06, UXR-09, UXR-10, UXR-12; BUG-2031, BUG-2044, BUG-2050, BUG-2061, BUG-2072. Inferred dispatcher steps (direct messages, phone fallback, relayed text updates) should be validated in dispatcher interviews. The enterprise competitor evaluation (UXR-04) is a single account, not a general churn trend.
